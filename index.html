Closure standard

When may I call an error fixed?

1 · RepairFix the smallest actual cause.
2 · ReattemptClose the source and reproduce the reasoning.

4 · Delayed retestProve the correction survives after time has passed.
60-second orientation

Powerhouse is not a timetable. It is an evidence-and-repair system.

The central question is simple: What is costing me marks, and have I actually fixed it? Everything else is secondary.

01

TEST

Attempt closed-book and under a real time limit. Keep the first attempt clean: no notes, Google or AI.

02

MARK

Mark strictly. Record raw score, confidence and guesses. A lucky correct answer is not secure mastery.

03

DIAGNOSE

Name the primary cause: knowledge, retrieval, concept, method, presentation, timing, attention or unsupported guess.

04

REPAIR

Repair the smallest thing that actually failed. Do not restart an entire chapter for one narrow error.

05

TRANSFER

Reattempt closed-book, then use a fresh question. Memorising the previous marking scheme is not transfer.

06

RETEST

Return after a delay. Only repeated clean retrieval closes the loop.

Use AI in the right phase

AI belongs after TEST.

Use AI to explain a diagnosed gap, compare your reasoning, generate a fresh transfer question or compress a correction rule. Do not use it to contaminate the first attempt.

Local-first privacy
